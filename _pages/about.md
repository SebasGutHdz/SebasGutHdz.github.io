Welcome! Thanks for checking out my website.

I am currently a fifth-year [CSE Ph.D.](https://math.gatech.edu/graduate/phd-computational-sciences-and-engineering) student (expected graduation: Fall 2026). I am seeking summer 2026 internships and full-time positions.

During my Ph.D., I have focused on two main research areas. My primary interest is developing computational methods for solving Optimal Transport problems with Lagrangian costs under the direction of [Prof. Haomin Zhou](https://hmzhou.math.gatech.edu/). Additionally, I have been working on Operator Learning techniques for solving PDE-constrained optimization under uncertainty with [Prof. Peng Chen](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~pchen402/).

In the summer of 2024, I interned at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL) under the supervision of [Dr. Mouhacine Benosmann](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cs7AJxcAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Dr. Saviz Mowlavi](https://www.merl.com/people/mowlavi).

As of Fall 2025, I am serving as President of the [SIAM Student Chapter](https://math.gatech.edu/graduate/georgia-tech-siam-student-chapter). If you are interested in being added to the mailing list or presenting, please reach out!

Outside of academia, I enjoy spending time in nature, cooking, and listening to music, particularly Japanese jazz. I maintain a curated playlist if you're interested!

You can find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebastian-gutierrez-9243b4114) and [X](https://x.com/Sebas_Gut_Hdz).
